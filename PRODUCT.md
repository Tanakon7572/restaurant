# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Restaurant staff at a mid-to-high-end establishment — one person often handles the full workflow: taking orders at the table, managing the menu, tracking statuses, and closing out. Used on a phone or tablet in ambient restaurant lighting, in short focused bursts between tasks. Speed and legibility matter more than discoverability.

## Product Purpose

An internal order management system for restaurant staff. Staff create orders, update statuses from pending through preparing to completed, and manage the menu (categories and items). Success means a single staff member can handle a rush with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

Refined, direct, trustworthy. The tool disappears into the task — staff don't think about the UI, they think about the order.

## Anti-references

- Generic dark SaaS dashboards (Vercel, Linear aesthetic): black surfaces, amber accents, glassmorphism cards
- Western white POS systems (Square, Shopify POS): high-contrast white, utility-first with no warmth
- Food delivery consumer apps (GrabFood, Shopee Food): loud red/orange, photo-heavy card grids designed for browsing

## Design Principles

1. **Legibility first.** Prices, statuses, and table numbers must be readable at a glance under dim restaurant lighting. No muted grays for data the staff needs to act on.
2. **Task cadence, not decoration.** Every visual element either carries information or confirms an action. Nothing is there for aesthetic filler.
3. **Consistent vocabulary.** The same button shape, same badge treatment, same price format across every screen. Staff shouldn't adapt to each page.
4. **Prices are primary data.** In the order-taking flow and menu management, price is the second most important piece of information after the item name. It should be treated as such visually.
5. **Earned confidence.** The tool should feel like it was built for this specific job — not a generic template that happens to contain menu items.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text must hit 4.5:1 against its background. Status badges and prices are operational data — they need sufficient contrast even in dim lighting. Support system dark mode (app is always dark).
